EDSA Inauguration

On 20th May 2026, the English Department Student Association (EDSA) officially inaugurated its 2026/2027 Executive Board at the SBSN Hall of Universitas Islam Negeri (UIN) Ar-Raniry, Banda Aceh. Carrying the theme "A Journey to Lead, A Commitment to Grow," the event marked the beginning of a new chapter for the organization, emphasizing leadership, commitment, and personal growth while strengthening its role in supporting English Education students.

During the ceremony, the organizing committee, former EDSA president, and university representatives shared inspiring messages about responsibility, collaboration, and the importance of prioritizing meaningful contributions within student organizations. They encouraged the new board to embrace challenges, foster solidarity, and continue building a positive impact on both students and the surrounding community.

The newly elected EDSA President for the 2026/2027 term, Alfaiz Rahmatillah, reaffirmed his commitment to making EDSA a welcoming platform where English Education students can learn, develop their potential, collaborate, and build lasting relationships. He expressed his vision of creating an active, innovative, and inclusive organization that empowers every member.

With renewed enthusiasm and a shared commitment to growth, EDSA aims to continue evolving into a progressive, inspiring, and impactful student organization.

February 01, 2026 Pidie Jaya Regency, Aceh Presidential

Care With EDSA

On January 31 – February 1, 2026, EDSA conducted a humanitarian visit to Pidie Jaya Regency to support communities affected by the flood disaster. This program was dedicated to delivering essential food supplies and school stationery, bringing care, encouragement, and hope to families and children in need.

Through this meaningful initiative, we strive not only to offer material assistance but also to nurture empathy, strengthen solidarity, and stand together with those facing difficult times. We believe that sincere compassion, no matter how small, can restore smiles and rebuild spirits.

May this journey remind us that humanity grows through helping one another, and may the spirit of giving continue to unite our hearts in kindness and gratitude.
